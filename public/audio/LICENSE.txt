OFFLINE DEMO AUDIO PROVENANCE

crystal.wav and happy.wav are locally synthesized pronunciations of two
original demo words, generated with FFmpeg's CMU Flite filter (voice slt).
No online TTS service or third-party human recording was used.
The speech engine is not bundled. Its permissive notice is retained below
for attribution. No endorsement by the engine's authors is claimed.
